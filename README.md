# Phytophysiognomies of Paraná

Thematic map of the phytophysiognomies of Paraná, Brazil, developed in QGIS for the Instituto Água e Terra (IAT).

The map was produced as a cartographic and educational resource to support communication, environmental education, and outreach materials developed by the institution. It aims to present the distribution of the main phytophysiognomies across the state in an accessible and visually clear format.

### Study Area

The map covers the state of Paraná and represents its main phytophysiognomic units, from Atlantic Forest.

### Data

The spatial datasets used in this project were obtained from IBGE, Google Earth Satellite and GeoPR Platform. 
The original source and licensing information for each dataset should be consulted according to its respective terms of use.
